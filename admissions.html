Admissions at Mini G Innova Spark

Learn about our application process, requirements and important dates for prospective students.

How to apply

We accept new students through a transparent admissions process that reflects our commitment to academic excellence and student wellbeing.

  1. Review admission criteria by grade.
  2. Complete the application form.
  3. Submit supporting documents.
  4. Attend the assessment and interview.

Important dates

Application opens: October 1

Assessment week: November 12–16

Offer letters: December 1

What we look for

Academic potential

Students who show curiosity, determination and readiness to learn.

Character

Respect, responsibility and kindness are central to our school community.

Engagement

Interest in clubs, events and extra-curricular learning is encouraged.
