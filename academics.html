Academic program

Our academic model combines strong classroom foundations with digital resources and student progress tracking.

Learning pathways

Students access subject notes, quizzes, assignments and results through the portal, whether they are in school or learning remotely.

Key academic features

  • Exam results workflow
  • Quizzes with XP rewards
  • Assignment tracking and deadlines

Subject resources

Maths, Science, English, Humanities and creative subjects are supported with downloadable notes and digital tools.

Student progress

Students can review recent quiz scores, assignment completion and academic achievements in one view.

Teacher support
